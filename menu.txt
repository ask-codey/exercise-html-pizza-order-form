SLICE OF HEAVEN PIZZA: WHAT THE ORDER FORM ASKS
================================================

The kitchen's computer reads each answer by its name, and each choice by its
value, so use the names and values below exactly as they are written.
The words in the left column are what the visitor reads: put them in labels.


SIZE: one per order                         name: size
  Small, 10 inches .......... $11            value: small
  Medium, 12 inches ......... $14            value: medium
  Large, 14 inches .......... $17            value: large
  Most people order Medium.

CRUST: one per order                        name: crust
  Hand-tossed                                value: hand_tossed
  Thin and crispy                            value: thin
  Chicago deep dish (+$2)                    value: deep_dish
  Gluten-free cauliflower (+$3)              value: cauliflower

TOPPINGS: any number, or none, $1.50 each   name: topping
  Pepperoni                                  value: pepperoni
  Italian sausage                            value: sausage
  Mushrooms                                  value: mushrooms
  Green peppers                              value: green_peppers
  Red onions                                 value: red_onions
  Black olives                               value: olives
  Pineapple                                  value: pineapple
  Fresh basil                                value: basil

HOW MANY PIZZAS: 1 to 10                    name: quantity

DELIVERY OR PICKUP: one                     name: delivery_or_pickup
  Delivery                                   value: delivery
  Pickup                                     value: pickup

READY BY: a time between 11:00 and 22:00    name: ready_time
  (11 a.m. to 10 p.m., when the ovens are on)

YOUR DETAILS
  Your name                                  name: customer_name
  Phone, in case the driver can't find you   name: phone
  Email, for your receipt                    name: email
  House number, for delivery (such as 42B)   name: house_number
  Street, for delivery                       name: street

NOTES FOR THE DRIVER: a few sentences       name: driver_notes
  Such as: "Blue door, around the back. The dog is friendly."
